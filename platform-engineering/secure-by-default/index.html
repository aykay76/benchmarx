Platform engineering

Secure and compliant by default.

Security that relies on every team remembering fails on a busy Friday. A platform can carry the controls, so the safe route is also the default one.

5 min read

Move the controls into the path

When each team implements security for itself, the result is uneven: some do it well, some do it late, and some skip it when the deadline is close. When the controls live in the platform, every service on the path gets them without having to ask.

Fewer secrets, shorter lives

The most effective habit is to remove long-lived credentials altogether. Workloads should prove who they are to the systems they call, using short-lived, automatically issued identities, rather than carrying keys that can leak into logs, images and chat.

Where secrets can't be avoided, they should live in one governed place, be scoped to one purpose, and rotate without anyone's weekend.

Evidence as a by-product

Auditors and regulators want proof that controls operated, not a promise. If delivery runs through a platform, much of that evidence can be produced automatically: who approved what, which checks ran, what was deployed and when.

Compliance evidence should fall out of how software is delivered, not be assembled in a scramble once a year.

Where this gets hard

What surprises teams after the launch

Scanners are easy to switch on and hard to live with. Without clear severity, ownership and a way to say "accepted risk", teams learn to ignore the noise, including the findings that matter.

Policies need tests, a trial period in audit mode before they enforce, and error messages that tell a developer what to fix. A control people can't understand becomes a control people disable.

Sometimes the right call is to bypass a control. Design that route in advance, with strong audit and a review afterwards, rather than letting people invent one during an incident.

Most of what runs in production came from somewhere else. Knowing what you ship, and being able to act quickly when a vulnerability is announced, depends on the groundwork being done beforehand.
